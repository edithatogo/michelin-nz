# Product Guide: Aggregate Gastronomy Indicator Dashboard

## Vision

The dashboard compares country-level fine-dining recognition against demographic and economic
context. It helps users reason about aggregate star density, GDP-normalized intensity, and New
Zealand's position without redistributing restricted guide records or review material.

## Current Product Surface

1. **Aggregate Country Metrics**
   - Country, ISO code, aggregate total restaurants, aggregate total stars, population, GDP, and
     calculated ratios.
   - No individual restaurant names, coordinates, reviews, booking links, or source rows.

2. **New Zealand Aggregate View**
   - A focused country-level explanation of New Zealand's current aggregate benchmark.
   - Methodology language makes clear that the site is not a replacement guide.

3. **GDP And Star Analysis**
   - Scatter and bar views comparing GDP per capita, stars per capita, and stars per economic
     output.

4. **Pivot Results**
   - Interactive aggregate table with filters and measure selection for country-level comparison.

5. **Sources And Licensing**
   - Source notes, redistribution boundaries, and limitations for aggregate analysis.

## Exclusions

The product excludes public restaurant-level records, third-party review data, proprietary guide
text, photos, location rows, booking metadata, and automated extraction from restricted sources.
